Manage Orders

Select an Amplify Price Quote to view and manage your allocation quantities.

Cards are grouped into Open, Submitted, Processing and Shipped columns; Cancelled requests are excluded. Each program is shown as its own tag on the card. Cards are not draggable — status is driven by the order lifecycle, not by user action. In this demo, Track Shipment opens the Shipment Status page and every other button and order number opens the Allocation Manager.
